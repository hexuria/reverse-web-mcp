what to believe

Reading it honestly

  • Samples are the difference that holds. One for ours on every task without a question. Four to six for B2 at depths three to six, fourteen at three hundred rows. B, one call per turn, is a sample per action.
  • Correctness is close once the harness is fair. B2 is 5/5 on the wait task and mostly right six deep. Our misses are the planner's: one empty intent on T3, one empty fork answer on T6, one payment-event timeout on T10, all scored as failures.
  • Width is not ours to claim. Parallel tool calls parallelise fine. What they cannot do is stop paying a thought per level.
  • Wall time is the planner's. Cheaper or cached planning is where the clock moves; the intent cache makes a repeat goal zero samples.
A retraction. The first campaign showed both loops at 0/5 on the wait task, and the README said the wait "separates the arms completely". That was a bug in our target app: a send arriving after the payment webhook overwrote "paid" with "sent", and only the slow arms ever sent late. Fixed, pinned by a test, and every loop cell on a wait task was rerun. The corrected cells are the ones on this page.
Arm A, the pixel loop, is smoke-only: 40 clicks per task, about 760k tokens, nothing finished, no coaching parity. Its screenshots are in the repo. It is the straw the design exists to avoid, and it is reported as the straw.
